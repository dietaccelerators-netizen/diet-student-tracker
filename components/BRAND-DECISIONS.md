Source: user-supplied Mastrevo_Brand_Guidelines_Review_Draft(2).pdf, 13 pages.

## Adopted for the portal
- Pages 3–5, 7–8 and 10: clear, professional, approachable identity; audience is adult ICAN/ATS students and working professionals.
- Exact colours: navy #08245C, bright blue #0759E6, ice #EEF6FF, white #FFFFFF. White reading areas/cards, ice background/callouts, navy structure/text/sidebar, blue primary actions and active states. Functional warning/error colours may remain for meaning.
- Use supplied wordmark and tagline “For Professional Accounting Exams”. Current web assets are direct extracts from the supplied PDF, without redrawing its artwork. Replace with original standalone vector/transparent assets when available. Do not stretch, recolour or reconstruct logo lettering.
- Page 6 proposes Inter. Use Inter as the working UI typeface; this remains a draft typography decision, not a claim of final approval. Desktop page headings 32/40 bold, section headings 24/32 semibold, reading text 16/24, control labels 14/20. Smaller supplementary labels are allowed. Use tabular numerals for tables/progress.
- One consistent outline icon family, with visible navigation labels. Keep seven existing portal destinations.
- Plain helpful copy, clear next steps, no promised exam success, guilt or forced slang.

## Carry forward

- Readable white lesson content, restrained panels and purposeful graphics. No decorative images inside syllabus/lesson/practice screens.
- When adding photos: natural adult learners/professionals, bright uncluttered settings. One subject identity image reused in its workspace header.
- Use app icon for browser/app identity when deployment metadata is revised; preserve supplied art. Standalone master artwork preferred over PDF extracts.
- Social posts, video thumbnails and downloadable planners should follow the brand when those outputs are actually built.
